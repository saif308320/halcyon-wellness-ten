The shop

A counter, a cellar, a name on the rib

Halcyon Wellness has traded from 41 Jermyn Street since 1872. The front room takes fittings. The cellar holds actions in file. Clients come with a certificate, or they do not come onto the floor.

We build side-by-sides and over-and-unders for driven days and walked-up ground. Stocks are cut from walnut we can name. Cases are oak and bridle leather, made in-house.

This is a licensed dealer’s shop. Proof, age, and paperwork are checked before a gun is shown. That is not a slogan. It is how the door works.
